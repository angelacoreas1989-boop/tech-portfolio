✦ About Me ✦

I’m Angela, and I love to learn about everything. Whether I’m learning a new system, exploring a language, or understanding someone’s point of view, I’m curious, learn quickly, and give my all to whatever I do. I’m pursuing a B.S. in Software Engineering at Western Governors University and bring more than 10 years of experience working with people in customer service, multifamily operations, centralized leasing, and systems support.

Connecting with people is one of my strengths. I get along with a wide range of personalities and adapt my communication to help each person feel comfortable, heard, and respected. I listen to understand, not simply to respond. When someone comes to me with a question or concern, I take time to understand what they need and follow through on the next steps.

My work has taught me to stay patient during difficult conversations, balance high-volume responsibilities, and bring care and accuracy to the details. I enjoy helping customers, collaborating with teammates, and working through problems together. I’m adaptable, dedicated, and willing to learn what’s needed to support the people who depend on me.

I bring that same mindset to technology. My projects draw on real customer and business workflows, small-business needs, and my own language studies. I speak English and Spanish and have elementary knowledge of Italian and Portuguese. Learning, building, and connecting with people are all part of the career I’m continuing to grow.
